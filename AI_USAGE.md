# AI Usage Declaration

## DCIT 208 Software Engineering Project

This document records the use of Artificial Intelligence (AI) tools during the development of the DCIT 208 project.

The team acknowledges that AI tools may be used to support learning, research, brainstorming, documentation, coding assistance, debugging, and other legitimate project activities. However, AI does not replace the team's understanding, decision-making, testing, or responsibility for the submitted work.

## 1. Team AI Use Principles

The team agrees to:

- Use AI as an assistance and learning tool rather than a replacement for individual understanding.
- Review and verify all AI-generated content before using it in the project.
- Test AI-assisted code before incorporating it into the project.
- Modify and improve AI-generated outputs where necessary.
- Ensure that every team member understands and can explain their own contribution.
- Maintain an honest record of significant AI assistance.
- Follow the academic integrity requirements of the DCIT 208 project.

## 2. Approved Uses of AI

AI tools may be used for activities including:

- Understanding software engineering concepts.
- Brainstorming project ideas and possible solutions.
- Explaining programming concepts and errors.
- Providing debugging guidance.
- Suggesting code examples or implementation approaches.
- Improving the clarity and grammar of documentation.
- Generating examples for learning purposes.
- Reviewing documentation and identifying possible improvements.
- Assisting with project planning and organization.
- Helping the team identify possible technical issues.

AI-generated suggestions will not automatically be accepted. Team members will evaluate and verify the information before incorporating it into the project.

## 3. Confidentiality and Data Protection

The team agrees that confidential or sensitive information must not be submitted to public AI tools.

This includes:

- Client passwords and credentials.
- API keys and access tokens.
- Private client records.
- Personally identifiable information.
- Confidential business information.
- Sensitive source code or proprietary information.
- Private documents that have not been approved for AI processing.

Where AI assistance is necessary, sensitive information will be removed, anonymized, or replaced with appropriate sample data.

## 5. Responsibility for AI-Assisted Work

Each team member remains responsible for their own contribution, including work produced with AI assistance.

Before submitting AI-assisted work, the responsible team member must:

1. Understand the work.
2. Check the accuracy of the information.
3. Test code where applicable.
4. Make necessary corrections or improvements.
5. Be able to explain the work to the team and lecturer when required.

A team member who cannot explain or demonstrate their own contribution may receive zero individual credit for that work, in accordance with the project requirements.

## 6. Academic Integrity

The team will not use AI to:

- Fabricate evidence or project results.
- Falsify client information.
- Create false meeting records or communication evidence.
- Misrepresent another person's work as our own.
- Submit AI-generated work that the responsible team member does not understand.
- Circumvent the learning objectives or assessment requirements of the course.

The team accepts responsibility for ensuring that AI-assisted work remains academically honest and properly documented.
