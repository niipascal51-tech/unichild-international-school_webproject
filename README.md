# Unichild International School Website and Student Information Management System

**Course:** DCIT 208 — Software Engineering
**Institution:** University of Ghana
**Team:** DevForge
**Project Status:** In Development

## 1. Project Overview

The Unichild International School Website and Student Information Management System is a software engineering project designed to provide a central online platform for school information and selected school management activities.

The proposed system will serve prospective parents, students, teachers, administrators, and the wider community by providing access to school information and authorized student services.

The project aims to improve access to school information and support the management of student admissions, academic results, fee status, school announcements, events, and other relevant records.

**School Motto:** Lógia Kai Práxeis — Words and Actions.

## 2. Project Objectives

The main objectives are to:

* Establish an online presence for Unichild International School.
* Provide information about the school, academics, admissions, and departments.
* Support administrator-controlled student registration and admission.
* Allow authorized students to access their academic results and fee status.
* Allow authorized teachers to view students and upload academic results.
* Support school news, announcements, events, competitions, and gallery content.
* Provide school contact information and relevant downloadable documents.
* Promote appropriate security and privacy for student information.

## 3. Main Website Sections

The proposed website includes:

8. Gallery
9. Contact Us

## 4. User Roles

### Administrator

The administrator manages student registration, admissions, student accounts, student records, academic results, fee status, announcements, and relevant website content.

### Teacher

Teachers log in to access permitted student information and upload academic results.

### Student

Students log in to access their own academic results, fee status, and other authorized information.

### Public Visitor

Visitors can view publicly available school information without requiring a student or staff account.

## 5. Student Admission Process

The required admission workflow is:

**Administrator registers student → Administrator admits student → Administrator creates student account → Student accesses the account.**

Students do not independently admit themselves into the school.
