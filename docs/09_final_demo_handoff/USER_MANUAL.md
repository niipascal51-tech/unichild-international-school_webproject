# Uni Child International School
# User Manual

**Project Team:** DevForge  
**System:** School Website and Student Information Management System

## 1. Introduction

This manual explains how users can access the Uni Child International School website and navigate the school management portal.

The system provides public school information and portal interfaces for administrators, teachers and students.

## 2. Accessing the System

Open the website using the following link:

https://zealous-flower-03357b203.4.azurestaticapps.net

A modern web browser and an internet connection are required.

To access protected portal features, use the appropriate account credentials supplied by the system administrator. Do not share passwords or leave an authenticated account open on a shared computer.

## 3. Public Website Features

Visitors can use the public website to explore:

- **Home:** General school information and announcements.
- **About:** Information about the school.
- **Academics:** Academic information.
- **Admissions:** Admission information.
- **News and Events:** School updates and activities.
- **Gallery:** School photographs and media.
- **Contact:** School contact information and enquiry options.

The availability of individual features may depend on the current deployment and configuration.

## 4. Administrator Portal

Administrators can access the administration interface using an authorised administrator account.

The interface includes school-management areas such as:

- Student management and registration.
- Teacher management.
- Results and examinations.
- Fee records.
- News, announcements and events.
- School content and configuration.

To register a student, open the student-management area, select the registration option, complete the required fields and follow the prompts provided by the application.

Only save genuine student information and ensure that access to personal records is restricted to authorised personnel.

## 5. Teacher Portal

Teachers should sign in with their assigned teacher credentials. Use the available portal options to access the teaching functions enabled for their account, such as relevant student or results workflows.

Only enter or modify records that you are authorised to manage.

## 6. Student Portal

Students should sign in using the credentials provided by the school administrator. Available functions may include viewing academic results and fee information, depending on account permissions and system configuration.

Contact the administrator if credentials do not work or expected records are missing.

## 7. Troubleshooting

**The website does not load**
- Check your internet connection.
- Refresh the page.
- Try a current version of another browser if the issue continues.

**Sign-in fails**
- Check the selected account role and credentials.
- Confirm that the account has been created and authorised.
- Contact the administrator if the problem persists.

**Records do not appear or update**
- Refresh the page and check your connection.
- Confirm that the relevant record has been saved.
- Report the issue to the administrator.

**A portal feature does not respond**
- Reload the page once.
- Record the action that caused the problem and any visible error message.
- Contact the project support person or designated system administrator.

## 8. Frequently Asked Questions

**Who can use the administrator portal?**  
Only users with authorised administrator credentials.

**What should I do if I forget my password?**  
Contact the designated system administrator for assistance.

**Can students edit official results?**  
Students should only access functions permitted by their assigned role.

**Who should handle technical problems?**  
Contact the school's designated system administrator or support contact.

## 9. Privacy and Security

- Use only authorised accounts.
- Keep passwords confidential.
- Do not share student information with unauthorised people.
- Sign out after using a shared device.
- Follow the school's applicable privacy and record-handling procedures.

## 10. Accessibility

Use a current browser, zoom the page when needed and navigate carefully through forms and controls. Report any barriers that prevent a user from accessing important features so they can be reviewed.
