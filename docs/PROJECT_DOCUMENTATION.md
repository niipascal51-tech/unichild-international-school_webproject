# Unichild International School — Project Documentation

## 1. Project Information

- **Project:** Unichild International School Website and Student Information Management System
- **Course:** DCIT 208 — Software Engineering
- **Institution:** University of Ghana
- **Team:** DevForge
- **Documentation Owner:** Documentation and Deployment
- **Status:** In Progress

## 2. Project Overview

The project aims to provide Unichild International School with a central online platform for school information and selected school management activities.

The proposed system will serve prospective parents, students, teachers, administrators, and the wider community.

## 3. Main Objectives

- Provide information about the school and its academic programmes.
- Publish admissions information, news, announcements, and events.
- Support administrator-controlled student registration and admission.
- Allow authorized students to access their academic results and fee status.
- Allow authorized teachers to view students and upload results.
- Support the management of school information and relevant records.
- Apply appropriate security and privacy controls.

## 4. Main User Roles

### Administrator
Manages student registration, admissions, student accounts, records, results, fee status, and relevant website content.

### Teacher
Views permitted student information and uploads academic results.

### Student
Accesses personal academic results, fee status, and other authorized information.

### Public Visitor
Views publicly available school information.

## 5. Student Admission Workflow

Administrator registers student → Administrator admits student → Administrator creates student account → Student accesses the account.

Students do not independently admit themselves.

## 7. Quality Assurance

Requirements should be linked to relevant development tasks, GitHub issues, code changes, reviews, and test evidence.

Testing claims must reflect actual results. A passing automated check does not prove that every feature works correctly.

## 8. Security and Privacy

Student records, academic results, and fee information must be protected through appropriate authentication, access controls, and privacy safeguards.

Passwords, API keys, and other secrets must not be committed to the repository.

## 9. Responsible Use of AI

AI-assisted contributions must follow the team's agreed AI policy and be recorded in `AI_USAGE_LOG.md`.

Contributors remain responsible for reviewing, testing, understanding, and explaining their work.

## 10. Project Status

The project is in development. Implementation, testing, deployment, and client acceptance must be verified through genuine evidence before being reported as complete.
